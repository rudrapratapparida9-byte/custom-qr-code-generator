About Custom QR Code Generator

Free, High-Resolution & Privacy-First QR Solutions

Custom QR Code Generator is a fast, versatile web suite designed to create crisp, customizable, and high-definition QR codes for websites, Indian GSTIN tax invoices, Phone Numbers, WhatsApp chat links, UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Wi-Fi network credentials, vCards, and plain text.

Key Features:

  • 100% In-Browser Privacy: Your payloads and contact credentials are never sent to external servers.
  • Vector & High-Res Output: Download in scalable SVG vector format or high-resolution PNG (up to 2048x2048 px).
  • Custom Styling: Personalize foreground/background colors, dot patterns, corner shapes, and center logos.
