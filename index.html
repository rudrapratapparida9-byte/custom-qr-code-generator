How to Generate Custom QR Codes for Photos, Documents, GSTIN & UPI

QR (Quick Response) codes provide instantaneous access to photos, PDF documents, websites, payments, and contact details. Our client-side generator allows you to create high-resolution QR codes that work smoothly with any smartphone camera.

1. Upload Photo / Image to Generate QR Code

Select the Photo / Image tab, upload your photo (JPG, PNG, WebP), and our generator instantly optimizes and encodes your photo into a scannable QR code image.

2. Upload PDF Document / Invoice to Generate QR Code

Choose the PDF / Document tab, drag & drop your PDF document, contract, or invoice to generate a direct QR code.

3. Indian GSTIN Tax Invoice & UPI Payment QR Codes

Easily generate GST-compliant QR codes by entering your 15-digit GSTIN, legal trade name, and invoice number. For contactless payments, enter your UPI ID (VPA) with an optional invoice amount to generate instant Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm payment codes.
